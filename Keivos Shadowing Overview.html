Mock-ups for the v1 build

Five pages, one per surface in the real product. Each is a working mock-up with its own sample data, pinned to Oct 25, 2026. State does not carry between pages. Buttons that would download or send show a message instead.

Students

Student app

Profile, email code, activation code or access request, find and filter physicians, book or request, waitlist, request more visits, cancel (late cancellations included), log hours, edit reflections, history and export, account and rules.

Open → Physicians

Physician portal

One Keivos account per physician, signed in by emailed code or from an email link. Overview with action and visit cards, Schedule with the calendar (availability shared or per organization), visit cards and collapsed history, My Organizations with per-organization settings and pause, My Profile with collapsed sections for practice locations, email preferences and activity.

Open → Physicians · invitation link

Join Keivos Shadowing

Guided onboarding from an organization's invitation link: use an existing profile or create one (verify identity, professional profile, connect, practice location, shadowing preferences, communication, availability), with Skip for now on everything after the profile and a setup checklist at the end. A scheduling coordinator can start it for a physician, who confirms by email. Physicians can also sign up without an invitation.

Open → Scheduling coordinators

Coordinator portal

One free account for office staff, authorized per physician and location. Overview and Visits & Requests across their physicians, Schedule with other locations shown only as Busy, Availability at their locations, Practice locations, Account with authorizations.

Open → Admin

Admin dashboard

Overview and action items (intakes, access requests, cap requests, attendance, reinstatement), physicians with a Recruitment view (invitation link and resources) and a tabbed read-only profile view, bookings, students with eligibility, analytics and term summary, settings in six collapsed sections including leadership permissions, organization profile and subscription.

Open → Email

Emails and confirmation pages

Every message the platform sends, to physicians, students and admins, rendered as received. Each button opens the one-click confirmation page it links to.

What the mock-ups stand in for

  • Exports, PDF downloads, outreach resources, the invitation link copy, "Recommend Keivos to an organization" and "Open my portal" show a message. Any six digits pass as an emailed code. The job that declines unanswered requests at visit start does not run in the mock-ups.
  • Photo upload is real in the browser (file picker, preview) but not saved anywhere.
  • Email buttons open the confirmation pages inside the Emails page; in production they are tokened links to the platform.
  • The i markers are the in-product help text. Hover or tap.
